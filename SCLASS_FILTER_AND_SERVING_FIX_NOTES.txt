Sclass Fitness App Patch

Fixed:
- Crohn's/IBS filter was too aggressive.
- Low FODMAP filter was too aggressive.
- Diabetic filter now removes obvious sugar drinks/candy but keeps normal lean proteins/veg.
- Lactose/dairy-free filter now removes obvious dairy unless labeled lactose-free/dairy-free.
- Halal/kosher filters remove obvious pork/shellfish but do not claim certification.
- Vegan filter remains strict because vegan-only should only show vegan-tagged foods.

Serving fixes:
- Bread/Ezekiel/Sourdough = 1 slice
- Deli meat = 1 slice
- Rice Krispies = 1 treat
- Rice cake = 1 rice cake
- Bagel = 1 bagel
- English muffin = 1 muffin
- Tortilla = 1 tortilla
- Wrap = 1 wrap
- Protein powders = 1 scoop
- Eggs = 1 egg
- Drinks = can/bottle or 1 cup depending item
- Fruits = each where normal

Important:
If your browser has old saved food data, click Reset Food DB once after loading this version.
